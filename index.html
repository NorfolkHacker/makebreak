// the whole idea, in one screen

~/manifesto — cat why.txt

You were promised a computer. You got a slot machine.

The feed's whole job is to keep your thumb moving and your hands empty — and lately half of what slides past is machine-made mush: posts nobody wrote, pictures nobody drew, made to be forgotten a second later. Junk food that snacks on you.

And no — before you sigh — this isn't an anti-AI rant. I lean on the stuff daily to skip the boring bits and build stranger things with the hours I claw back, and I automate half my recon with it. The line I care about isn't AI or no-AI. It's this:

Same lump of silicon: in the maker's hand it's a workshop, in the consumer's hand it's a doomscrolling distraction.

So MAKE / BREAK is me stubbornly staying on the workshop side of that line, out loud, every other week — and dragging a few mates back over with me.
