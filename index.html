// the whole idea, in one screen

~/manifesto — cat why.txt

You were promised a computer. You got a slot machine.

The feed is engineered to keep your thumb moving and your hands empty. And now the timeline is filling with frictionless, soulless, auto-generated slop — text nobody wrote, art nobody made, all of it optimised to be scrolled past.

We're not against the tools. We automate the boring parts and build weirder things with the time we win back. The dividing line isn't AI — it's this:

A power tool for people who make things.
A pacifier for people who scroll.

Same silicon. Opposite lives. MAKE / BREAK is for the first kind — and it's a running invitation to drag your friends back across that line.
